Limits we state

What the tool does not claim.

Bright stars saturate. Brighter than about J = 10.5 the core is clipped: no SPHEREx spectrum, and the motion is measured on a wider field.
Asteroids to about V 19. Fainter known bodies are not recovered inside a pass yet; a track needs four visits.
A lead is not a discovery. Anomalies and candidates are leads until another telescope confirms them.
A search has a depth. Below it, finding nothing does not show that nothing is there; no search here claims Planet X.
