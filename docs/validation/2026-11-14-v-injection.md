`validation/v_injection.py` on the white stacks of three real 20′ fields (ε Indi B, 4 passes; Barnard's Star and WISE 0855−0714,
3 passes): four point sources at a time (FWHM 8.8″, peak a multiple of the stack's robust noise, straight motion at a random direction
from a random place), 20 per field and cell; recovered when a track passes within 3″ of the places in ≥ 3 passes with a speed within

Reading: a mover as bright as the field's clear stars (peak S/N ≥ 40) is found in about 85 % of cases at any speed; at S/N 20 it is
found but often only as a weak three-pass track, which the page does not draw; below S/N 12 the search misses most movers (each pass
must detect it at 8 robust σ). The chance control re-runs the search with the moving sources of each pass at random places: 0.0
