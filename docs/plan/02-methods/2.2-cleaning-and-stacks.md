# 2.2 Cleaning and stacks (super-frames)

All moving pictures rest on one product: for every pass, one clean stack per detector and per named band on a common north-up grid,
with the saturated share and the weight. Raw frames do not show motion: each exposure sees the target at another wavelength, angle and
pixel grid, so stepping through raw frames shows the blink of the spectrum and the jitter of pixels, not a shift of a third of a pixel.

## 2.2.1 Background

- Per exposure: subtract the 3-iteration 3σ clipped median of the window.
- From 10′ up: also subtract what depends on wavelength alone, the median in **40 wavelength bins** (the helium airglow line at
  1.083 µm is a stripe of +1.4 MJy/sr across D1; without this the cosmic-ray test takes ten times more pixels).
- ZODI is **not** subtracted for pictures (white frames with and without ZODI differ by 0.5 % of their noise at 5′); it is subtracted
  for the distant-body products and the photometry windows that read it anyway.
- No flat-fielding, background modelling or PSF matching beyond the SPHEREx pipeline's.

## 2.2.2 Masks

`mask = finite(img) & !sat_core & !(flags & BAD_ALWAYS) & !((flags & BAD_FAR) & r_target > 6 px)` (2.1.3). A pixel outside an
exposure's footprint is "no data".

## 2.2.3 Super-resolution stack (splat)

The 6.15″ pixel is larger than a star image and the exposures of a pass are dithered at random, so each native pixel deposits its value
onto a finer grid and normalisation is done over all exposures at once (interpolating each exposure first, then averaging, blurs).

```
S   = grid step (1.5375″ up to 20′, 3.075″ at 60′)
for exp in exposures of (pass, detector):
    u, v = sky_to_grid(exp.ra_pix, exp.dec_pix) + registration_shift[pass]
    deposit exp.weight * exp.img bilinearly on the 4 grid points around (u, v) into NUM; exp.weight into DEN
NUM, DEN = gauss(NUM, sigma), gauss(DEN, sigma)        # one smoothing of the sums, same Gaussian
stack = NUM / DEN;  weight = DEN
```

| Parameter | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| kernel σ, stack with ≥ 16 exposures | **0.35 detector pixel** | sharpest without a lattice: white-frame FWHM of isolated stars 10.7″ → 9.2″ (live 8.8–9.1″) |
| kernel σ, stack with < 16 exposures | **0.50 detector pixel** | thin stacks at 0.35 show a weight lattice above 3 % (161 of 873 stacks, all < ~15 exposures); at 0.30 the pass difference on empty sky has rms 0.089 against a noise floor 0.054 (lattice blinks) |
| count rule for a named band | median exposures per detector pixel | |
| display smoothing | 0.5 grid pixel | applied by the renderer, not stored |

## 2.2.4 Cosmic-ray hits: leave-one-out test

A hit is present in one exposure with nothing at that sky place in the others; small (137 of 160 traces one pixel); sharper than a
star in D4–D6 but not in D1–D3; unflagged; of any brightness (up to 600 MJy/sr in wide fields); more frequent in long-wavelength
detectors (of 195 rejected: D4 35, D5 40, D6 117). L.A.Cosmic (astroscrappy) was tested and rejected: with a pixel this large it flags
32 thousand star pixels per exposure in D1–D3.

```
for exp in exposures of (pass, detector):              # streaming, one round with online updates (2.2.10)
    others = (NUM - deposit(exp)) / (DEN - weight(exp))   # the stack WITHOUT this exposure, inside its footprint
    peak   = max_filter(others, 1 native pixel)
    sigma  = 1.4826 * MAD(exp.img[sky])
    hit  = (val - max(o, 0) > 8*sigma) & (val > 5*max(pk, 0))      # brighter than anything the others see
    hit |= (o - val > 8*sigma + 5*abs(o))                          # or a dip
    keep_source = (o > 5*sigma) & (val < 20*pk)                    # a source is never rejected as a hit
    reject = hit & ~keep_source
    remove the rejected pixels' deposits from NUM, DEN at once
```

- The comparison must be with the stack **without** the exposure (a 596 MJy/sr trace raised the common stack and produced a false
  track of 4.3″/yr).
- Source protection: injected hits rejected 0.65 / 0.74 / 0.81 at 2 / 10 / 50 MJy/sr on empty sky; a hit on a star core is let through
  (50 MJy/sr: 7 % rejected) — the price of never rejecting a source (the core of ε Indi B is up to 10× brighter in some D1 exposures).
- Rejected pixels are kept as a list of single-exposure detections (they can be a fast mover).
- Validation: Barnard's Star narrow field 165–195 pixels rejected (D6 most); ε Indi B ~800–934; 1° fields 23 thousand (0.02 %);
  no star core rejected.

## 2.2.5 Streaks and blotches of one visit (fields ≤ 12′)

Some D5/D6 visits of ε Indi B carry a faint streak (0.1–0.2 MJy/sr, 3–4 px wide, > 25 long) along the row toward ε Indi A; cause not
established; unflagged. Per (pass, detector) with ≥ 3 visits:

```
img[v] = splat(visit v);   ref = percentile(img, 30, over visits)
d = gauss(img[v] - ref, 1 px) - median;   r = gauss(ref, 1 px) - median
core = grow(r > 5*noise(r), 1 px)                      # source cores are not checked
odd  = ~core & (|d| > 2*max(r, 0))                     # a wing changes with wavelength, not by a factor of three
drop = regions of (odd & |d| > 3*noise(d)) with max |d| > 5*noise(d) and size >= 16 detector pixels
```

Validation: ε Indi B 41 visits, 5,691 pixels (0.32 %), 80 % in D5/D6; Barnard's Star 2 visits, 321–477 pixels; measured target shifts
unchanged (4.58 and 10.40″/yr). Not applied above 12′ (fainter than the noise in a white frame there).

## 2.2.6 Halos of bright stars

Diffraction spikes and rings turn with the roll angle between passes and imitate sources and motion.

```
zones = connected(sat > 0.5);  real = [z for z in zones if area(z) >= one detector pixel]   # one source at the zone centre
halo  = gauss(minimum_filter(stack, 25″), 12″);   hl = max(halo - median(halo), 0)
accept a source only if (sm - halo > 8*noise) and (sm - halo > 3*hl)
```

A hard radius mask fails (ε Indi B lies in the halo of ε Indi A at 1°). Validation: ε Indi 1°: 5 tracks → the 2 real ones; Barnard 1°: 1
track either way, threshold raised over 1.4 % of the area (6.6 % for ε Indi).

## 2.2.7 Saturation, white frame, colour

- Saturated share per grid pixel: `sat = Σw_sat / (Σw + Σw_sat)` over the detectors of the white frame only; `sat > 0.5` → **white**
  on screen. (Computed over all detectors, a detector with 3–4 exposures turns isolated spots white far from stars.)
- **White frame** of a pass = mean of the detector stacks over `CDET`, the detectors present (≥ 4 exposures) in **every** pass;
  otherwise the wavelength mix changes between passes and the whole field "breathes". ε Indi B: CDET = D2, D3, D5, D6.
- **Spectral colour** stacks: R, G, B channels from the six detector stacks with a 3×6 matrix built from `lam_rgb(λ)` at each
  detector's wavelengths (2.3.2), so colour needs no re-stacking.

## 2.2.8 Registration

Passes are registered on pass 0 using field stars of the two white frames (centroids, median shift), applied as a cubic-spline shift of
the smoothed NUM and DEN (they are band-limited by the smoothing, so this equals depositing at shifted positions). Validation:
residual 0.03–0.05″ (Barnard), 0.04–0.10″ (ε Indi, five usable stars).

## 2.2.9 Field sizes and grid rule

| Field | Grid step | Grid size | Per-visit test | Wavelength background | Query points for the index |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2′, 5′ | 1.5375″ | ~80, ~200 | yes | no | 3×3 |
| 10′ | 1.5375″ | ~400 | yes | yes | 3×3 |
| 20′ | 1.5375″ | ~800 | no | yes | 3×3 |
| 60′ | 3.075″ | ~1170 | no | yes | 5×5 |

- At most **800 exposures** per field; above that (R Draconis near the ecliptic pole, 2617) the set is thinned evenly keeping every
  pass and detector, and the status line says so.
- Wide stacks are for display, not astrometry: the shift of Barnard's Star from a crude centroid on the 60′ stack is 9.4″ against
  10.2″ from the narrow frames.
- At 60′ all six detectors in all passes cover ~96 % of Barnard's field; ε Indi D2, D3, D5, D6 cover 85 %.

## 2.2.10 The streaming build and the quick frame

The build never holds all exposures in memory:

1. **Quick part first:** 2–4 exposures per pass and detector (4 up to 10′, 3 up to 20′, 2 at 60′), white light only, kernel 0.5
   detector pixel with 4 per detector (0.7 with 3, 1.0 with 2), cosmic-ray test only. Shown white (no colour) until the full frame
   arrives, because a quick colour changes static stars between passes.
2. **Per pass:** every exposure is read once into the sums of its detector; then read again from the window cache (disk, ~0.01 s) and
   tested against the others with the leave-one-out test (a rejected pixel leaves the sums at once); the surviving pixels go into the
   final sums of the detector, the seven named bands, the saturated share and the flag census. The pass is smoothed, registered on
   pass 0, written as a block of the output parts, and dropped. The next pass is read while the current one is stacked.
3. Fields ≤ 12′ also run the per-visit test (2.2.5) with the exposures of one (pass, detector) together.

Equivalence: the streaming build and an in-memory build agree to 1e-4 MJy/sr on the 6′ Barnard field. Memory: one 60′ field of 603
exposures peaks at 1.1 GB and falls back to ~290 MB. Payload: Picture part (R, G, B float16 + validity + saturated share) 0.9 MB at 5′,
14.8 MB at 20′, 33 MB at 60′ for 3 passes (gzip 0.6 / 10.2 / 23 MB); all parts of a 60′ field 245–490 MB (section 5.7 of the
architecture: production sends the Picture part first, others on demand, and a lighter 60′ representation).

Timing (measured, link-bound, residential): cold 5′ quick 10 s / all 41 s; 20′ quick 22 s / all 125 s; 60′ quick 40 s / all 700 s.
Warm opening of every test field under 2 s (slowest 1.87 s, R Draconis 60′).
