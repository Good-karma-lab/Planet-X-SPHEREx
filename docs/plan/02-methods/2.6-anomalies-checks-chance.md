# 2.6 Anomalies, identification and chance controls

## 2.6.1 The spectral anomaly map

Which sources of a field do not have the spectrum of an ordinary star of that field? A background job on a finished field, started by
one visible button ("Find spectral anomalies here") with smooth progress.

```
sources: stationary sources of the search between passes with S/N ≥ 12 (brightest 300, saturated left out) + every track (measured
         along it: otherwise its spectrum is truncated and it is anomalous for a false reason)
measure: PSF flux of every source in every exposure of the field (≤ 800; OUTLIER kept for tracks); the file is opened once per
         exposure and all sources are measured from it (windows from the field's cache)
bin:     24 bins in ln λ from 0.75 to 5.0 µm: median and 1.25·MAD/sqrt(n); a source needs 60 points and 20 bins
shape:   f / Σf
family:  med = median shape of the bright half; pc1, pc2 = first two principal components of the bulk (temperature and reddening move
         a stellar spectrum along them)
fit:     model = a·(med + c1·pc1 + c2·pc2)
score:   sqrt(mean(((shape − model)/sqrt(e² + (0.05·model)²))²))      # 5 % floor keeps bright stars' systematics out
anomalous: score > median(score) + 6 · 1.4826 · MAD(score);  < 12 sources with a spectrum → "no ordinary star can be defined"
per source: resid_z (24 numbers) and the up-to-3 driving bins (largest shares of the squared residual)
```

The **anomaly passport** says why in status form: score against the field's threshold; "excess at 3.9–4.3 µm (+22σ, 16 % of the
misfit)", "deficit at 0.80 µm (−20σ)"; the residual per bin on the chart against the ordinary star; whether it moves or changes; and
what other catalogues know (2.6.2). "Anomaly n", never "discovery".

Validation: ε Indi 20′: 65–74 sources, threshold 1.19–1.38 (median 0.46–0.48, robust scatter 0.15), **ε Indi B first by far**
(score 11.4–11.8; the next 3.0); drivers excess 1.5–1.7 µm (+27σ), 3.9–4.3 µm (+22σ), deficit 0.80 µm (−20σ). Barnard 20′: 275–344
spectra, 10–11 above threshold, the strongest are Gaia M dwarfs at parallax 5–8 mas among distant giants. Limits: a 5′ field has
~7 sources (no ordinary star); a field of one kind of star (a cluster) flags more; a blend, a variable or a bad detector can make an
anomaly; time ~2 min per 350 sources on one core (research).

## 2.6.2 Identification and context (catalogue cross-checks)

**Identify (what a catalogue calls a place at a date):** the nearest SIMBAD object within 4–6″ of the place at the epoch (its J2000
place moved by its proper motion; planets skipped), else the nearest Gaia DR3 star (G < 17.5, moved from 2016.0). Fast movers: SIMBAD
asked in two cones (radius + 1″/yr × years; and radius + 11″/yr × years for objects above 1″/yr), so Barnard's J2000 place 4.5′ away is
found. Outcomes are explicit: a name; "not in SIMBAD or Gaia" (both answered, nothing there); "catalogues not reachable" (never cached).
The match radius is 4″ for a click (8″ named a faint 9σ source after WISE 0855, J = 25, which SPHEREx cannot see: wrong).

**Context (what else is known about a place):** asked in parallel with a 12 s deadline each; every part has a state `ok` / `none` /
`unavailable` (with the error); `ok`/`none` cached 7 days, `unavailable` never cached. Surveys are searched at their own epoch (place
moved by the matched Gaia or SIMBAD proper motion).

| Part | Fields |
|---|---|
| SIMBAD | name, object type, spectral type, bibliography count and link, proper motion, parallax, magnitude |
| Gaia DR3 | source id, G, BP−RP, parallax ± error, RUWE, pm, variability flag |
| 2MASS PSC | designation, J, H, Ks, quality |
| AllWISE | W1–W4, cc_flags |
| CatWISE2020 | W1, W2, proper motion |
| AAVSO VSX (VizieR B/vsx/vsx) | name, type, period |
| NED | name, type, redshift |

**Distance and tiers** (Object card): distance from Gaia DR3 parallax only when RUWE ≤ 1.4 and parallax ≥ 3 mas; otherwise a SIMBAD
parallax labelled less certain for the target, none for a clicked star. Tier A: clean Gaia star (RUWE ≤ 1.4, parallax ≥ 3 mas,
unsaturated); B: identified but saturated, or no clean Gaia match; C: no catalogued source within 2.5 px (only brightness can be said).
Speed across the sky = 4.74047 · μ(″/yr)/parallax(″) km/s. Known exception: RW Aurigae's SIMBAD basic parallax (15.42 mas) is wrong;
the Gaia DR3 value of RW Aur A (6.41 mas, RUWE 1.5) is used and the card says the matched Gaia source is RW Aur B.

**Known bodies:** IMCCE SkyBoT cones (geocentric, moved to the spacecraft viewpoint) for tracks inside a pass or visit; JPL Horizons for a
named body (observer `500@-163182`); JPL SBDB for the orbit, H, diameter; JPL Small-Body Identification for "who was in this field".
A found track is matched to a known body only after the search.

## 2.6.3 Chance controls (every search has one)

| Search | Control | Rule | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| between passes | moving sources placed at uniform random places per pass (stationary kept), the linker re-run, ≥ 20–60 trials → mean tracks per field, P(any), by track length | draw a track only when chance makes < 0.15 tracks of its length per field; replaces the `weak` rule of thumb | designed and measured in research (mean 0–0.73 tracks per field on six 20′–60′ fields) but too slow (2–2000 s); **production target < 5 s per field** (vectorised linker, ≤ 150 moving sources per pass, 20 trials) |
| pairs | the pair test on a sample of 150 pairs of steady sources 3.5–12″ apart → false-pair rate × candidates | show expected false pairs beside the found ones | designed, not built |
| inside a pass (film) | candidates moved to chance places, times kept, linked and recovered identically (12–20 trials); control photometry 40″ aside | < 0.15 tracks of that length per field | built in research: 0 of 50–64, chance 0.0 for ≥ 4 visits |
| inside a visit | exposure times permuted within each visit, ≥ 100 trials of the whole linker | false-alarm probability < 1 % per track | built in research |
| beyond Neptune (one pass, deep sum) | negated differences set the ceiling; photometry of the negated peaks sets the threshold; control tracks 1′ aside | peak > 1.05 × best negated; photometry ≥ 5σ; control tracks < 5σ | built in research: 0 of 64 control tracks reached 5σ |
| anomaly map | robust threshold median + 6σ of the field itself | — | inherent |
| Difference | z calibrated on empty sky to width 1 | |z| > 4 coloured, not a detection | built |

Every lead card shows its chance number beside its significance, and the Movers row shows the chance numbers per scale.
