## Description

SPHEREx Sky is a free web tool that plays real images from NASA's SPHEREx space telescope as a moving picture, so anyone can see how a patch of sky changes and spot what moves.

Try it, no account needed: https://spherex.space

What the video shows (every picture is a screen recording of the live tool on real SPHEREx data):
0:00 Barnard's Star, pass by pass: the search finds it moving, 10.7 arcseconds a year, and Gaia agrees (10.4).
0:07 ε Indi B, a cold brown dwarf, found moving and red among the white stars.
0:13 Find Planet X: exposures added along every orbit from 30 to 2000 au, to look for slow bodies beyond Neptune.
0:21 Eris found blind: 95.6 ± 0.4 au from three passes linked (JPL 95.5). Sedna: 83.0 au, a strong lead.

SPHEREx maps the whole sky in 102 infrared colours between 0.75 and 5 micrometres and comes back to every place about every six months. Raw frames show colour flicker, not motion, so we clean, stack and colour every pass, then play the passes like a blink comparator and search them for movers. Every find is a lead with its evidence, never a discovery.

Challenge: Planet X and SPHEREx, NASA International Space Apps Challenge 2026.
Team: Good Karma Lab, Barcelona Local Event. Aleksandr Ostapenko (team owner), Katherine Karmatskikh.
