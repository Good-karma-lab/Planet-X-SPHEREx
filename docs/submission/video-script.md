# Demo video: 30 seconds

At most 30 s, English subtitles burned in. Every picture is a screen recording of https://spherex.space on real SPHEREx data, made by a
scripted browser (`web/scripts/demo-video.mjs`, cut by `demo-cut.mjs`); no generated imagery and no synthetic voice, so no SI watermark is
needed. It leads with what moves between the passes and with Find Planet X. Numbers on screen are the live ones of the Method page.

|---|---|---|

| 7–13 s | Lab, ε Indi B 20′: the brown dwarf moving among white stars | A cold brown dwarf, ε Indi B, found moving and red. |
